Your FeesConnect account.

What the website stores

Registration stores your name, email address and a protected password hash. Verification and password-reset links expire. Your account stores the support requests you submit and any payment records later created by connected services.

How information is used

Account details are used for sign-in, verification, password recovery and responding to support requests. Account email is delivered through the website’s configured email provider. Your password is never sent in an email.

Cookies and sessions

A necessary, HTTP-only session cookie keeps you signed in. Sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity or 12 hours overall. You can sign out at any time. Changing or resetting your password signs out other sessions.

Payment demonstration

The demonstration is separate from your account. Use sample details there. It stores demonstration records in the browser tab and does not process real payments. Never enter card numbers, security codes or banking passwords into the demonstration or support chat.

Questions about your information

Log in and submit a support request for questions about your account, corrections or deletion. Connected payments are completed on the payment provider’s website. Pupil links are assigned after verification; provider confirmation, settlement and school allocation are recorded separately.
